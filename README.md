# Hi Recruiters

## What is this?

A physics simulation of the three-body problem - the classic chaotic system where three masses orbit each other under gravity which is not predictable by any equation and even a small change can completely change the trajectory 

## Why build this?

I wanted to understand computational physics beyond just reading about it. The three-body problem is famous for being analytically unsolvable, so you have to simulate it numerically. this made me understand that science simulation is difficult and require detailed attention
